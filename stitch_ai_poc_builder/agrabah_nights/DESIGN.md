## Brand & Style

The visual identity marries the enchantment of an Arabian night with the rigor and clarity of an enterprise-grade SaaS application. The aesthetic draws deep inspiration from the royal architecture of Agrabah, swirling genie sorcery, and desert dusk—reinterpreted through a modern luxury glassmorphism lens. 

Key attributes:
- **Tone:** Prestigious, mystical, empowering, and exceptionally polished.
- **Visual Motif:** Deep celestial midnight skies, radiant genie-smoke cyan illumination, and royal gold accents reminiscent of inlaid brass and treasure chambers.
- **Experience:** High-contrast legibility paired with ethereal depth. Surfaces mimic frosted lapis lazuli and tinted obsidian glass edged with subtle gossamer-gold filaments. Interactions carry fluid, smoky transitions that feel effortless and magical without sacrificing product velocity or accessibility.

## Colors

The palette operates in a default dark mode, constructing an immersive midnight realm punctuated by vibrant bioluminescent glows and gilded metallic warmth.

- **Primary (`#0DBEB2` - Genie Cyan):** The primary beacon for interactive states, key actions, live status indicators, and focus focus rings. Evokes mystical energy and boundless possibility.
- **Secondary (`#EAB308` - Lamp Gold):** Reserved for badges of prestige, premium tiers, warning highlights, active golden borders, and high-value conversion elements.
- **Tertiary (`#7C3AED` - Magic Carpet Violet):** Serves as an atmospheric accent, providing gradient stops, ambient edge backlights, and secondary tags that deepen the royal nocturnal aesthetic.
- **Neutral (`#0B0C1E` - Arabian Midnight):** The foundational substrate. Deep blue-black canvas surfaces elevate into tinted sapphire tiers (`#131533` for surface containers, `#1B1D44` for elevated dialogs and cards).
- **Text & Foreground:** High-luminance off-whites (`#F8FAFC`) ensure AAA reading accessibility against dark backgrounds, while soft desert gold sands (`#FEF3C7`) are utilized for secondary metadata and luxury subheadings.

## Typography

The type system is built entirely on Plus Jakarta Sans, combining crisp geometric clarity with refined humanist curves that harmonize with modern interface design while providing warmth and approachability.

- **Display & Headlines:** Tightly tracked headings feature heavy weights (`700` and `800`) to evoke a sense of architectural grandeur and decisive authority.
- **Body:** Neutral weights (`400`) balanced with generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) ensure long-form data dashboards, analytical reports, and workflow panels remain effortlessly scan-friendly.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Enhanced letter-spacing on small caps and badges provides structured, crisp legibility even when rendered in shimmering golds or vibrant genie cyans over dark canvas planes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an 8px spatial grid with fluid page frameworks:
- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views employ a responsive 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 32px (`2rem`) margins, expanding to 48px margins on ultrawide viewports (>= 1440px). Tablets step down to 8 columns with 16px gutters, while mobile adopts a 4-column framework with 16px margins.
- **Rhythm & Safe Areas:** Vertical spacing between modular card clusters scales using `space-lg` and `space-xl`. Complex data-dense views (such as data tables or canvas editors) compress padding strictly down to `space-xs` and `space-sm` for high-efficiency scan paths.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons flat gray drop shadows in favor of tinted luminous depth, drawing upon Agrabah’s twilight atmosphere and frosted midnight glass.

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - *Base Layer:* `#0B0C1E` pure canvas.
  - *Mid-Tier Containers:* Semi-translucent midnight blue (`rgba(19, 21, 51, 0.75)`) layered over a 16px background blur.
  - *Floating Dialogs & Popovers:* Elevated sapphire obsidian (`rgba(27, 29, 68, 0.85)`) with an integrated 24px blur.
- **Luminescence & Shadows:** Depth is articulated through multi-layered ambient light:
  - *Low Elevation:* `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(11, 12, 30, 0.8), 0 0 0 1px rgba(234, 179, 8, 0.08)` (subtle gold ambient halo).
  - *High Elevation / Floating:* `0 16px 36px -4px rgba(7, 8, 20, 0.9), 0 0 24px rgba(13, 190, 178, 0.12), 0 0 0 1px rgba(234, 179, 8, 0.2)` (genie-smoke cyan refraction combined with imperial gold wireframes).
- **Outlines:** Borders are always low-opacity tinted gradients (such as golden sand feathering into transparent midnight), avoiding harsh single-pixel white lines.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a balanced curvature (`roundedness: 2`), reflecting the elegant arches of Agrabah’s palatial architecture without becoming overly toy-like.

- Standard inputs, buttons, and control elements utilize `0.5rem` (8px).
- Cards, modal windows, and flyout panels utilize `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px).
- Highlight hero segments and pill badges take on `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) or full capsule radiuses for status indicators.
- Decorative framing avoids harsh rectangular joins in favor of soft, pillowed edges that mimic carved polished gemstones.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Genie Magic"):** Rich cyan gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #0DBEB2 0%, #0F968D 100%)`) with dark navy typography (`#0B0C1E`, `font-weight: 700`). Interactive hover yields an ethereal cyan outer glow (`0 0 18px rgba(13, 190, 178, 0.45)`).
- **Secondary ("Sultan Gold"):** Translucent deep navy base with a crisp golden border (`1px solid rgba(234, 179, 8, 0.5)`) and sand gold text (`#FEF3C7`). Hover invokes a gentle amber surface wash (`rgba(234, 179, 8, 0.1)`).
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Pure transparency with muted cyan-slate text (`#94A3B8`), shifting to bright cyan text on interaction.

### Input Fields
- Dark sapphire translucent fill (`rgba(19, 21, 51, 0.6)`) with a delicate baseline border (`rgba(234, 179, 8, 0.2)`).
- On focus, transitions cleanly into an active cyan perimeter glow (`0 0 0 2px rgba(13, 190, 178, 0.5)`) and shifts background opacity upward. Text is crisp off-white, with placeholders tinted in sand beige (`rgba(254, 243, 199, 0.4)`).

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed from midnight sapphire glass with a 1px dual-stop border gradient running from translucent gold at the top-left to deep magic violet at the bottom-right.
- Provides a sense of tangible luxury while allowing underlying dashboard data streams to remain coherent.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact capsules with radial tinted backdrops.
- Status tags feature tiny inner light nodes: Cyan for "Active/Wish Granted", Gold for "Imperial/VIP", and Purple for "Automated/Magic Run".

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Box elements leverage an inset midnight base with a subtle gold perimeter. Checked states fill with vibrant cyan and display a sharp midnight-blue glyph, backed by a micro radial glow.

### Specialized Component: "Wish Bar" (Command Palette)
- A floating global omnibar with high-blur backdrop (`backdrop-filter: blur(28px)`), framed with an animated ambient hairline of gold and violet. Houses quick shortcuts, natural language queries, and workflow triggers.
